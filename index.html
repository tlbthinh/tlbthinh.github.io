Hello! I recently completed my M.S. in Computer Science at the University of Dayton, OH, working in the Vision and Mixed Reality Lab with Prof. Tam Nguyen on computer vision, multimodal learning, and generative AI. Before that, I spent three years as a backend software engineer at Shopee, an experience that makes me care about methods that hold up outside the lab, not just on benchmarks. My recent work uses generation both as a tool for models — synthesizing adverse weather to stress-test perception — and as a subject of study in itself, from detecting AI-polished reviews to building an open-source AI coding tutor.

I am open to both research and engineering opportunities where these interests meet.
